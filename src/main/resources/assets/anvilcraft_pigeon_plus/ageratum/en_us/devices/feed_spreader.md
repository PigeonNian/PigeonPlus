# Feed Spreader

<block id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:feed_spreader"/>

The feed spreader is driven by falling anvils. When the anvil presses down, the top bucket rotates and spreads items around it.

## Inventory

- Right-click with food or bone meal in hand to insert it.
- Right-click with an empty hand to take it out.

## Range

The effect diameter is determined by the falling height of the anvil. The maximum radius is 4 blocks, producing a 9x9 square area.

- Bone meal grows plants in range. Each successfully processed plant has a 30% chance to consume bone meal.
- Food feeds breedable animals that are not in breeding cooldown.
