# Nozzle

<item id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:nozzle"/>

The nozzle converts mixed combustion events triggered inside a large cauldron into nozzle exhaust. It must be installed on the corresponding side of the large cauldron, and its facing determines the exhaust direction.

## Ignition

The nozzle does not directly consume ordinary fuel. Instead, it checks whether the large cauldron is currently triggering mixed combustion.

- Oil and liquid oxygen form kerosene exhaust when both are present and ignited.
- Liquefied biogas and liquid oxygen form methane exhaust when both are present and ignited.
- The exhaust shuts down when the nozzle or any sub-block receives a redstone signal.

## Center Jet

The center exhaust continuously provides momentum to entities. When used with a stasis beacon locking a falling anvil, the momentum is accumulated during time freeze and released all at once when stasis ends.

- Kerosene exhaust can accelerate up to about 320 b/t over 30 seconds.
- Methane exhaust can accelerate up to about 192 b/t over 30 seconds.
- The center tail flame can ignite wooden blocks and heat heatable blocks to incandescent.

## Power and Heating

When nozzle exhaust passes through a valid structure, it can heat nearby blocks and collect charge for magnets. Its structure is similar to a plasma jet, requiring magnets and heatable blocks to be placed offset from each other.

<structure id="../structures/nozzle_plasma.nbt"/>

Specifically, the plasma jet generated by the nozzle has no directional requirement. It has usable faces whether it fires upward, downward, westward, eastward, or in any other direction.

Note that the exhaust can reach about 15 blocks at most, but only the first 12 blocks can actually be used for ion jet effects.

Therefore, nozzle exhaust can theoretically produce up to about (6x12)x(512+256) = 55296 kW of power, but due to the efficiency limits of charge collectors and heat collectors, some efficiency is lost unless infinite collectors are used.
