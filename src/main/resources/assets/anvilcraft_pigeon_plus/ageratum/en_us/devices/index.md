This chapter covers the machines added by this mod.

# Devices

- [Nozzle](nozzle): converts mixed combustion in the large cauldron into a directed gas jet.
- [Stasis Beacon](stasis_beacon): freezes an entity or a falling block inside its beam.
- [Anvil Pump](anvil_pump): driven by a falling anvil to pump fluids and gases.
- [Feed Spreader](feed_spreader): spreads feed or bone meal when pressed by an anvil.
- [Blender](blender): blends fluid ingredients below a cauldron.
