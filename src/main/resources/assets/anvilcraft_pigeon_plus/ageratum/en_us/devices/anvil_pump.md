# Anvil Pump

<block id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:anvil_pump"/>

The anvil pump is driven by falling anvils. It can work with the fluid network to input or output fluids, or to compress gases.

## Gas Liquefaction

When a pump continuously inputs gas into a large tank already filled with the corresponding gas, the gas is converted into a liquefied product at a fixed ratio.

- Biogas can become liquefied biogas.
- Compressed air can become liquid oxygen.
- Both normal pumps and piston pumps can drive liquefaction.

## Air Intake

When all six faces around a drain are not blocked, the pump can extract compressed air from the drain. Pipes and pumps are ignored by this exposure check.

Gas sent into a drain enters storage first, then escapes from the drain and creates particles with the corresponding color.
