# Blender

<block id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:blender"/>

The blender is used to process biomass-related fluids and serves as the starting point of the biogas production chain.

## Mixed Biomass

Crops and other materials can be converted into mixed biomass through blending. Mixed biomass can then participate in reactions inside a large cauldron to produce biogas.
