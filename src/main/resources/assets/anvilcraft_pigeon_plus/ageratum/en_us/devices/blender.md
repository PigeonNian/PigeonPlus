# Blender

<block id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:blender"/>

The blender is used to process biomass-related fluids and serves as the starting point of the biogas production chain.

## Mixed Biomass

Crops and other materials can be converted into mixed biomass through blending. Mixed biomass can then participate in reactions inside a large cauldron to produce biogas.

<item id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:mixed_biomass_bucket"/>

## Compressed Air

The blender only runs while it is being fed **compressed air** from its input side, and it consumes **20 mB of compressed air per tick** while blending. It stores up to 1000 mB of compressed air internally, so pump compressed air into the input face to keep the machine working.
