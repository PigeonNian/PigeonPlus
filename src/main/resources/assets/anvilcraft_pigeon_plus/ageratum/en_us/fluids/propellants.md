# Exhaust and Fuel

Nozzle exhaust is triggered by vaporization events in the large cauldron. The nozzle only checks whether the event exists, making it more compatible with other sources.

## Kerosene Exhaust

When oil and liquid oxygen are both present and ignited, kerosene-type exhaust is triggered. This exhaust has a higher acceleration limit, and its visual flame tends toward a bright white-orange color.

## Methane Exhaust

When liquefied biogas and liquid oxygen are both present and ignited, methane exhaust is triggered. This exhaust has a lower acceleration limit, and its visual flame includes a sky-blue outer flame.

## Consumption

Fuel and liquid oxygen are continuously consumed even when the nozzle is not working.
