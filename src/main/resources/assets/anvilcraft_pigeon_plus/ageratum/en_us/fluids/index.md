This chapter covers the fluids added by this mod, and the unique behavior of gases inside pipes.

# Fluids

- [Mixed Biomass and Biogas](biogas): biomass into biogas, its mob-repelling effect and liquefaction.
- [Liquid Oxygen and Compressed Air](oxygen): extracting compressed air and liquefying it into liquid oxygen.
- [Exhaust and Fuel](propellants): making liquid oxygen, plus the recipes, ignition and consumption of the three nozzle exhaust types.
- [Liquid Chocolate](chocolate): melting and solidifying chocolate blocks, plus blending recipes.
- [Gases in Pipes](gas_diffusion): gases diffuse by pressure, unaffected by gravity.
