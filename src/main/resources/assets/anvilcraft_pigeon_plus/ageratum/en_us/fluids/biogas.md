# Biogas

<item id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:gaseous_biogas_bucket"/>
<item id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:liquefied_biogas_bucket"/>

Mixed biomass can be converted into biogas. Biogas can be stored in large tanks and further liquefied into liquefied biogas.

## Gas Behavior

When stored as a gas, biogas can escape through drains. When the contents are biogas, monsters will not spawn in a 15x15x15 area around the drain, and a range outline similar to an induction light is displayed.

## Liquefaction

When biogas is continuously pumped into a large tank already full of biogas, the biogas is converted into liquefied biogas according to the compression ratio.

Liquefied biogas can be ignited together with liquid oxygen to trigger methane-type nozzle exhaust.
